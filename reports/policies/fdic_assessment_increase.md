# Higher FDIC deposit-insurance assessments

Policy `financial_services/fdic_assessment_increase` · reviewed 2026-10-31 · **FAIL-UNRESOLVED**

## A. Mechanics and scope

Configured mechanics below are a model specification, not a certification of current law. Current legal thresholds, exemptions, effective dates and phase-ins have not all been independently verified. Treat unresolved legal scope as a failure, not an implicit approval.

Higher recurring FDIC deposit-insurance assessment rates raise costs at insured bank subsidiaries. The assessment base is average consolidated bank assets less average tangible equity, not insurer premiums or only insured deposits. Consolidated-company exposure estimates must isolate the insured depository subsidiaries.

Duration: `permanent`. Default inputs: `{"d_rate_bp": 2.0}`.
