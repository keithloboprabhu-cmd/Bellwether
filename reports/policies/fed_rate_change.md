## A. Mechanics and scope

Configured mechanics below are a model specification, not a certification of current law. Current legal thresholds, exemptions, effective dates and phase-ins have not all been independently verified. Treat unresolved legal scope as a failure, not an implicit approval.

The Federal Reserve moves short-term US rates and the curve shifts in parallel. Rate cuts squeeze net interest income at asset-sensitive deposit banks (Wells Fargo, Bank of America, JPMorgan); Citi's US dollar book and Amex barely move.
