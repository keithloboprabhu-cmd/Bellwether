# Property tax increase

Policy `real_estate_hospitality/property_tax_change` · reviewed 2026-10-31 · **FAIL-UNRESOLVED**

## A. Mechanics and scope

Configured mechanics below are a model specification, not a certification of current law. Current legal thresholds, exemptions, effective dates and phase-ins have not all been independently verified. Treat unresolved legal scope as a failure, not an implicit approval.

Higher property tax rates raise operating costs for owners of real estate. Net and CAM leases pass most of it to tenants at Prologis and Simon; tower owners and owned hotels absorb more; Airbnb's hosts, not Airbnb, pay tax on listed homes.

Duration: `permanent`. Default inputs: `{"d_tax_pct": 10.0}`.
