# Interest rate change

Policy `real_estate_hospitality/interest_rate_change` · evidence checkpoint 2026-11-01 · **FAIL-UNRESOLVED**

## A. Mechanics and scope

Configured mechanics below are a model specification, not a certification of current law. Current legal thresholds, exemptions, effective dates and phase-ins have not all been independently verified. Treat unresolved legal scope as a failure, not an implicit approval.

A parallel move in short and long rates reprices floating-rate and maturing debt, changes interest earned on cash, and moves REIT property values through cap rates. REIT GAAP EPS understates the hit (property value carries it), and Airbnb's large cash pile means higher rates help it.

Duration: `permanent`. Default inputs: `{"d_bps": 100.0}`.
