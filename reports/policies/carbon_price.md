# Carbon price on direct emissions

Policy `energy_resources/carbon_price` · reviewed 2026-10-31 · **FAIL-UNRESOLVED**

## A. Mechanics and scope

Configured mechanics below are a model specification, not a certification of current law. Current legal thresholds, exemptions, effective dates and phase-ins have not all been independently verified. Treat unresolved legal scope as a failure, not an implicit approval.

A US price per tonne on each company's direct (Scope 1) CO2e emissions. Refiners and producers pass part of the cost into fuel prices; NextEra's regulated utility (FPL) recovers most of it through rates; copper and crude are priced globally, so FCX and COP absorb most of theirs. Covers Scope 1 only - an upstream tax on the carbon in fuels sold (Scope 3), which would cut oil demand far more, is not modeled here.

Duration: `permanent`. Default inputs: `{"price_per_ton": 50.0}`.
