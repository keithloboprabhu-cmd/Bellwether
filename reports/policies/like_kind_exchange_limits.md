# Limit 1031 like-kind exchanges

Policy `real_estate_hospitality/like_kind_exchange_limits` · reviewed 2026-10-31 · **FAIL-UNRESOLVED**

## A. Mechanics and scope

Configured mechanics below are a model specification, not a certification of current law. Current legal thresholds, exemptions, effective dates and phase-ins have not all been independently verified. Treat unresolved legal scope as a failure, not an implicit approval.

Taxing part of the gains now deferred through Section 1031 exchanges makes private property buyers pay less, nudging cap rates up and REIT property values down. Exposure in this universe is small and value-only; REITs themselves rarely rely on 1031, and the hotel companies and Airbnb own little property.

Duration: `permanent`. Default inputs: `{"taxed_share_pct": 75.0}`.
